echoes of the fractured core

In the year 2147, the planet Erebus shattered under the weight of its own overmined core. What remains are colossal floating archipelagos—jagged islands of rock suspended in toxic storms, riddled with ancient mining veins of volatile crystite, the last resource fueling fusion reactors and plasma weaponry. You command one of two rival Vanguard factions, crash‑landed exiles from a fallen interstellar empire. Your opponent leads the opposing Vanguard, both of you scrambling to establish dominance on a single, contested archipelago. Crystite is finite—scattered in depletable veins across the map—and every extraction counts. The islands are a deadly chessboard. Position your turrets on high ground for kill zones, cluster generators for energy shields, and erect barriers to funnel enemy fire. Resources dwindle with every build; overextend, and your hub flickers out. Waves of feral stronghold drones—remnants of Erebus's automated defenses—erupt periodically from neutral enemy citadels dotting the map. These relentless swarms target the weakest base, buying time for your rival to strike. Outmaneuver your foe through superior positioning: claim chokepoints, deny veins, and bait stronghold assaults onto their lines. Alliances are illusions—only one hub survives the storm. Forge your legend, or become dust in the void.
